Why an inspection matters

The leak is almost never where the stain is

By the time water shows up on your ceiling, it has already traveled — running along a rafter, pooling in the decking, soaking into insulation you can't see. The stain is just the exit point. The real problem is somewhere upstream, and staring at the ceiling will never tell you where. A 20-minute look from the roof side will — whether you're dealing with a quick, cheap fix or something that's quietly getting worse, and more expensive, every time it rains. That's the whole reason to get eyes on it now instead of wondering.
