Quick questions

Is the inspection really free?

Yes. The 23-point roof inspection and the quote are completely free, with no obligation. If your roof is fine, the pro will tell you that.

Will I get blown up by 15 companies?

No — that's the whole point. Your details go to one vetted local pro, not a dozen. We never sell your info to a room full of salespeople.

Are you a roofing company?

No. Service Matchup is a free matching service. We connect local homeowners with independent, licensed, insured local roofing professionals — and we only hand you to one.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Your matched pro can inspect for storm and hail damage and document it with photos, which is what you need before you file. Depending on the damage, you may qualify to have work covered — the inspection tells you where you stand.

Is financing available?

Yes — many of our matched pros offer flexible financing options so a needed repair or new roof can fit your budget. Ask about it during your free inspection.

Am I obligated to buy anything?

Never. Get the free inspection, get your answer, and decide on your own time. No pressure, no door-knockers.
