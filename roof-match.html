Straight answers

The questions homeowners actually ask.

Is the inspection really free?
Yes. The 23-point roof inspection is free and there's no obligation attached to it. The roofer earns your business by doing good work on the quote, not by charging you to look at the roof. There's no charge for the match either.
Will fifteen companies start calling me?
No — and that's the whole point of ServiceMatchup. Your information goes to one vetted local roofer. We don't sell it to a list of contractors and we don't auction it off. One pro, one call.
Are you a roofing company?
No. ServiceMatchup is a matching service, not a roofing contractor. We don't perform roofing work. We match homeowners with independent, licensed and insured local roofing professionals, and all work is performed by and contracted directly with that independent pro.
Can you help with an insurance claim?
The pro we match you with can inspect and document the roof's condition before you file, which is what most homeowners are missing when a claim goes sideways. Depending on what they find and what your policy covers, you may qualify for coverage — but that's your insurer's decision, not ours and not the roofer's. We don't file, adjust, or guarantee claims or any outcome, and you remain responsible for any applicable deductible.
Is the financing real, or a bait-and-switch?
It's real, and it's offered by the contractor and their lending partners — not by us. Many matched pros have monthly payment options available. Terms, rates, and availability vary by provider and are subject to credit approval, so nobody can promise you a specific number before you've talked to them.
Am I obligated to anything if I fill this out?
Not at all. You're requesting a free inspection and a quote. You can take the report, get a second opinion, or do nothing with it. No deposit, no contract, no pressure.
How fast will I hear back?
Usually the same day. Your matched pro reaches out to schedule a time that works for you — often from a local number, so keep an eye out for it.
What's actually in a 23-point inspection?
The pro walks the roof and checks the things that predict failure rather than just what's visible from the street — shingle condition and granule loss, flashing, valleys, penetrations, ridge and vents, gutters, decking, and attic-side signs of moisture. You get photos of what they find and a plain-English explanation.
